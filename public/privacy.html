Privacy policy

CAT Sprint · Last updated 11 November 2026

In short: we keep what is needed to sign you in (your email address, and your name and profile picture if you use Google) and a copy of your study progress, so it follows you to every device. We don't sell or share your data, show ads or track you.

Questions or deletion requests: yugayush123@gmail.com

Who we are

CAT Sprint is a free web app for preparing for the CAT exam. "We" means the people who build and run it. You can reach us at the email above.

What we collect

  • Email sign-up: your email address and password. The password is stored only as a secure hash by our sign-in provider, so we can never see it.
  • Google sign-in: with your permission, Google shares your email address, name and profile picture with us. We do not get access to your Gmail, Drive, contacts, calendar or any other Google data.
  • Sign-in records: our sign-in provider keeps basic security logs, such as when you signed in and from which IP address.
  • Study progress: your answers and times, scores, bookmarks, notes, error log, test results, mock logs and plan progress, saved to your account.
  • Crash reports: if the app breaks while you're signed in, it sends us the error message, the page name, your browser type and the app version, linked to your account, so we can fix it. They contain none of your answers or notes.
  • Page-view counts: we count visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies. It records the page, the site you came from, and your country, browser and device type. It doesn't store your IP address or recognise you across days.

What is kept on your device

Your progress is also saved in your browser's local storage, so the app works offline and syncs when you reconnect. Your sign-in session, your light/dark setting and, before you create an account, your answers to the three warm-up questions on the home page are kept there too. The warm-up answers stay on your device; if you then sign up on it, they move into your account.

How we use it

  • To create your account, sign you in and keep you signed in.
  • To save your progress and show the same progress on every device where you sign in, kept separate for each account on a shared device.
  • To find and fix bugs (crash reports) and see which pages are used (page-view counts).
  • To send emails needed for your account, such as confirming your email address. We don't send marketing emails.

Where it is stored and who can see it

Accounts, progress and crash reports are stored with Supabase, on servers in Mumbai, India. The site is hosted by Vercel, which also provides the page-view counts. If you choose Google sign-in, Google handles that step. The app's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address when the page loads. We do not sell, rent or share your information with anyone else, and we use no advertising tools.

Google user data

CAT Sprint's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We use your Google email, name and profile picture only to sign you in, and for nothing else.

Cookies

We don't use tracking or advertising cookies, and our page-view counting is cookieless. The app uses your browser's local storage only for your sign-in session, settings and study progress.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Your account stays until you ask us to delete it. Email us from the address on the account and we will delete it, with its saved progress and crash reports, within 30 days.
  • Your study progress is erased everywhere with Your data → Erase all progress in the app. Clearing this site's data in your browser removes only the copy on that device.
  • Google access can be removed at any time from your Google Account permissions.

Children

CAT Sprint is meant for people preparing for the CAT exam and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update it here and change the date at the top.
