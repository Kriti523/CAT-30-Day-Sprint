# 30-Day Study Plan

Availability assumed: **weekdays 2 h (120 min) · Saturday 4 h (240 min) · Sunday 5 h (300 min) · busy-day fallback 45 min**. Day 1 is a Monday (set your own start date in the app). Question references (Q001–Q011, D07, R03, V075–V077) point at the in-app question bank; every reference is checked by `npm test`.

Generated from `src/data/plan.ts`, the same data the app uses.
